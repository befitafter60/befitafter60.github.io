General Fitness

A complete workout for everyday life: strength, balance, endurance, flexibility and mobility, three mornings a week.

Stronger After 60

Focused on building strength, starting with a 5-minute flexibility warm-up. Twice a week, for people who want to get noticeably stronger.

What you need

A computer, tablet or smartphone with Zoom, a sturdy chair, and a resistance band. Joe will help you get set up, even if you've never used Zoom before.
